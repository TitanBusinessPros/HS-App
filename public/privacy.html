Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This policy describes information handled by the Homesteader application operated by Titan Business Pros LLC.

Information handled

  • Google sign-in information, including your account identifier, name, and email address.
  • Information you submit, such as your introduction, location, website and social links, photos, questionnaire answers, selected skills and experience, messages, and interview details.
  • Application records used for review and safety, such as submission dates, review status, admin access, and account blocks.
  • Interview booking invitations, admin bookable hours and blocked times, and confirmed appointments in Homesteader.
  • Technical information processed by the hosting, sign-in, and security services needed to operate the app, which may include device or network information.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate users, review applications, contact applicants, arrange interviews, maintain the homestead page, screen submitted text and photos, and protect the application from misuse.

When an applicant books an interview, Homesteader saves the confirmed time on the applicant profile and sends a private message. Applicants see only available booking times, not the admin's other appointments. An interviewee may see the contact admin's email address.

Who can access information

Within the app, an applicant can view their own private application and conversation. Authorized Homesteader admins can review applicant information and messages. Applicant profiles and conversations are not shown in a public directory.

Google services process information needed for Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check and reCAPTCHA Enterprise, and Google Cloud text and photo screening. GitHub Pages serves the public app files. See Firebase privacy and security information for more about Google's services.

Storage and retention

Private application records, including uploaded photos, are stored in Firestore. The installed app may cache public pages and other static files for offline fallback; it does not put private application records in that cache. No automatic expiration date is set for application records. You may contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion. Service-provider logs or backups may follow their own retention practices.

Admins can change their bookable hours, block times, and cancel booked interviews in Homesteader. Booking does not connect to or update an outside calendar.

Your choices

You can return to update your profile, questionnaire answers while the questionnaire is available, and selected skills. The app limits changes to an existing photo set to once every 30 days. Contact us if you want help with your information or account. Visiting outside links, including our Facebook page, is subject to those sites' own privacy practices.

Changes and contact
